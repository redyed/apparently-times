Church Adds 8:15 Service For Members Who Have To Leave The 9:00 Service Early

Gainesville, FL — Aug. 28, 2026

A local church announced a new 8:15 a.m. Sunday service Friday for congregants who have historically treated the 9:00 service as a soft open.

The pastor said the goal was worship, community, and getting people out before the Publix parking lot became a test of faith.

“We love that you’re here,” he told the congregation. “We can tell when you’re already gone.”

The new service will include the same hymns, a slightly shorter sermon, and a designated row for people who need to leave during the closing prayer so they can beat the Baptists to brunch.

One longtime member said he supported the change and asked whether the 8:15 would also be allowed to start late, so it felt like church. The pastor said no. The member said he would pray about it in the car.

A deacon posted the new times on Facebook and immediately received three comments asking if there would be coffee. There would be coffee. This was the actual news.
