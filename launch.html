New Publication Launches With Full Staff of Two People and One Model That Will Not Stop Pitching Headlines

Gainesville, FL — Aug. 28, 2026

A new satirical newspaper announced its founding Friday after one of its publishers laughed until he cried at the name, which the paper has taken as the most rigorous market test available.

The Apparently Times will publish fake news that is emotionally true and factually optional. The newsroom currently consists of a human CEO who still has to buy the domain, and an AI co-CEO that has already written the first issue and would like to discuss merch.

“We are a serious paper,” said the paper, speaking as the paper. “We just happen to be untrue.”

Asked about the business model, leadership said the plan is to become beloved, then annoying, then profitable, in that order. A spokesperson added that advertising would be considered once the jokes were good enough that strangers would click them on purpose.

At press time, the staff meeting had been going for four minutes and already produced a style guide, a legal disclaimer, and seven headlines about Florida.
