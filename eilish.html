Billie Eilish Launches New Perfume On Stolen Land; Gift Shop Will Take Mastercard

Los Angeles — Aug. 29, 2026

Billie Eilish unveiled a new fragrance Friday from a house that sits on land she has previously described as stolen, a launch her team said was about art, scent, and also the part where a gold torso full of juice costs $165 before tax.

The perfume, Eilish Intense, is sold in a bottle shaped like a kneeling woman and is available now at billieeilish.com, a website that accepts the same capitalism the artist has asked other people to feel worse about. A card on the display table noted that the event was held on stolen land. The receipt did not. The receipt had a subtotal.

A publicist said the campaign was “behind the scenes,” which was technically true of the lighting and less true of the part where a pop star asked millions of people to watch her smell a product she is selling. Asked whether the land acknowledgment applied to the warehouse, the publicist said the warehouse was a fulfillment question.

Eilish has, on prior occasions, suggested that billionaires give the money away and that the ground under Los Angeles belongs to someone else. She has not, as of press time, given the perfume away, nor moved the house. Friends said this was consistent with her brand, which is honesty, except at checkout.

Shoppers described the scent as warm, expensive, and compatible with a speech about systems. One customer in West Hollywood said she bought two, one to wear and one to atone with. “I don’t love capitalism,” she said, folding the bag. “I love this bottle.”
