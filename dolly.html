National Hurricane Center Clarifies Tropical Storm Dolly Has Nothing To Do With Dolly Parton, Country Immediately Unconvinced

Miami, FL — Aug. 28, 2026

The National Hurricane Center issued an unusual statement Thursday explaining that Tropical Storm Dolly was named from a rotating list and is not a meteorological tribute to the late Dolly Parton.

The clarification appeared to have the opposite effect.

“That’s exactly what they would say,” said a woman in Jacksonville, loading water into a cart that already contained batteries, white bread, and a sense of narrative.

Local meteorologists pleaded with viewers to focus on wind speed. Viewers focused on whether the storm would have the courtesy to go around theme parks.

A man in Gainesville said he was taking the storm seriously, but not so seriously that he wouldn’t make the joke. He then made the joke. His wife said that was the last one. It was not.
